Rule used to decide pause vs. trade: an hour is paused only once it shows
BOTH a real net loss AND a depressed win rate, with a reasonable sample
size behind it -- not just whichever hour happened to be red on one night.
Two hours that LOOKED great (02:00-03:00 AM, +2.24 SOL; 11:00 PM-12:00 AM,
+1.50 SOL) were deliberately left untouched/not favored: each one's entire
gain came from a single trade, and without it both were flat (~+0.04 SOL).
22:00-23:00 (10-11 PM) is the one hour that's genuinely, broadly good even
with its best trade removed -- notably the hour immediately AFTER the
9-10 PM pause window, a real sharp swing hour-to-hour, not a smeared trend.
